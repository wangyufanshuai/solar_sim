# Mainline A — A3 nebula manifest audit (v607)

Six first-set nebulae now have reviewed CC BY 4.0 image rights and exact local JPEG deliveries: M42, M1, M8, M16, M20, and M57. The six files total **1,971,256 bytes**. The other six first-set nebulae remain catalog markers with image rights pending: NGC 7293, NGC 6543, NGC 2237, NGC 6960, Carina, and B33. This is partial first-set coverage; image delivery does not itself complete visual or performance acceptance.

The item-level [rights review](./mainline-a-stage-a3-rights-review.json) records the published [ESA/Hubble reuse policy](https://esahubble.org/copyright/), every image's full credit, the page's policy link, and the absence of an explicit item-level exception. The policy explicitly covers named third-party contributors unless stated otherwise. Every use must retain the complete unaltered credit visibly, link to the source and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and describe significant subsequent visual changes. Reuse does not imply endorsement.

The [local delivery manifest](./mainline-a-stage-a3-local-delivery.json) binds each object ID and image ID to its local path, source page, exact official screen JPEG URL, credit, license, dimensions, byte count, and SHA-256. These official renditions are 1280 pixels wide; their heights vary. They are not the full-resolution originals. The delivered bytes are unchanged from the individually reviewed screen JPEGs.

The historical [acquisition receipt](./mainline-a-stage-a3-image-acquisition.json) remains unchanged. It records the earlier in-memory-only fetch with `localPayload.acquired=false`, `licensed=false`, and `awaiting-rights-review`. The later rights review and local delivery are separate records. `node scripts/acquire-mainline-a3-image-receipts.mjs` checks that historical receipt offline when it already exists; do not refresh it to describe the later delivery.

`node scripts/materialize-mainline-a3-assets.mjs` verifies the six files against that acquisition receipt, downloads any missing file, and emits the local delivery manifest, runtime asset registry, and visible-credit instructions. Existing JPEG files are verified rather than overwritten. `node scripts/verify-mainline-a1.mjs` checks matching item-level clearance, local bytes and SHA-256, acquisition identity, and coverage counts.

The runtime registry is `app/data/mainlineANebulaVisualAssetsV607.generated.ts`; the original candidate identities are retained in `app/data/mainlineAFirstSetNebulaCandidatesV607.ts` and the [candidate audit](./mainline-a-stage-a3-candidate-audit.json). Metadata reports six `licensed` assets, zero pending source-page candidates, and six `catalog-marker` objects. Each licensed image carries its documented bands and a no-naked-eye representation claim. Science mode keeps the catalog marker separate from the observation image; the image does not become a measured three-dimensional structure.

Their catalog names are `IC 1805`, `IC 1848`, `IC 2118`, `IC 2944`, `IC 405`, `IC 410`, `IC 417`, `IC 443`, `IC 5146`, `M17 / NGC 6618`, `NGC 2024`, `NGC 2070`, `NGC 2174`, `NGC 281`, `Rho Ophiuchi`, `Sh2-235`, `W40`, and `Cepheus C`. None matches the first-set A1 nebulae. Their NASA public-use candidate labels remain review states; this six-image clearance does not alter them.
