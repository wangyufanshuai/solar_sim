4. Carina Nebula (`nebula:carina`) - Massive southern star-forming region
5. Ring Nebula (`nebula:m57`) - Planetary nebula close target
6. Vela Pulsar (`pulsar:b0833-45`) - Nearby bright pulsar marker
7. Alpha Centauri (`star:alpha-centauri`) - Nearest bright stellar system
