Curated Sky Atlas visual navigation export. Coordinates and distances are for Solar Sim exploration, not certified astrometry.

1. Orion Nebula (`nebula:m42`) - Existing project deep-sky asset
2. Pleiades (`cluster:m45`) - Curated star-cluster catalog
3. Crab Nebula (`nebula:m1`) - Curated nebula catalog
4. Carina Nebula (`nebula:carina`) - Curated nebula catalog
5. Ring Nebula (`nebula:m57`) - Curated nebula catalog
6. Vela Pulsar (`pulsar:b0833-45`) - Curated pulsar catalog
7. Alpha Centauri (`star:alpha-centauri`) - Nearby star catalog
8. Vega (`gaia-star:vega`) - Gaia DR3 bright-star cross-match
